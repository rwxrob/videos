# Please join me for morning IRL walks and "standups"

In tech, the morning "standup" is a time to share what you did yesterday, what you will do today, and anything that is blocking you so that people can possibly help you outside of the meeting to overcome those blockers. What if we did that every morning, but for things related to your personal life?
