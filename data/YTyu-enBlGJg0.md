# 🥹 I'm sorry nano

Offers an apology for mocking nano users and argues that every beginner should learn it, since it is often already installed and can open unexpectedly, such as when editing a crontab. Also explains why Ctrl key habits picked up in nano can lead beginners to freeze their terminals unless the shell is configured to prevent it.

My sincere apology. Learn nano, sometimes it is all you have.
