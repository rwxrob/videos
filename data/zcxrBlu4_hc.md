# Fantastic weekend with nephew mountain biking in Bryce Canyon.

I'm not going to lie I feel less inclined to livestream than I've ever felt in my entire life. I'm not even going to take my cameras or streaming equipment with me in the car. I'll keep it around for a while and decide if I want to use it maybe next year but the truth is I'm probably not.

I know I've said all this before. But something has significantly changed inside. I can't really explain it but I hope other people have it happen to them.

I know that there are lots of people that have grown to depend on our community for friendship and support and I don't know what to say. Keep looking. Find people who aren't behind a computer screen. Go outside. Be real. Make friends with the butterflies and birds if you have to. I do.  I've never felt alone on one of these solo bike packing trips and it's even better with family and friends.

As much as I hate Facebook that's where I will be documenting adventures. It's just the easiest and that is where all the bikepicking communities are. Friend me there, or not. I post most things publically.
