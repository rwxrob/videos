# 📺 Gruvbox terminal with Ubuntu Mono font on Mac

Walks through installing a Gruvbox dark theme in the built-in Mac Terminal app, setting it as the default, installing the Ubuntu Mono font from Google Fonts, and increasing the font size. Also explains why the built-in Terminal is preferred over alternatives such as Alacritty, Kitty and iTerm2, mainly for its speed and availability.
